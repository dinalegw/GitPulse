# Monetization Status

GitPulse does not currently ship billing, subscriptions, paid accounts, or hosted repository automation. The local CLI and public playground do not check a user's plan.

`gitpulse-website/lib/entitlements.ts` is roadmap scaffolding for possible Free, Pro, Team, and Enterprise offerings. Those definitions are not products for sale and do not prove that the related features, retention guarantees, workspaces, SSO, or elevated limits exist.

Before monetization launches, GitPulse must implement and verify the underlying service, pricing and checkout, webhook idempotency, subscription lifecycle, authorization at every server-side action, customer data deletion, support policies, and truthful plan documentation. Until then, all tier descriptions should be treated as proposals rather than current commitments.
